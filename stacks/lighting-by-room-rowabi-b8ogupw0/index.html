Rowabi LightingRowabi Lighting is a Texas-based lighting brand focused on bringing thoughtfully designed, high-quality lighting into everyday homes. Headquartered in Houston, Rowabi combines craftsmanship, modern aesthetics, functionality, and accessible pricing to create fixtures that feel both distinctive and practical. The brand is especially known for its rattan and woven lighting collections, along with pendant lights, wall sconces, flush mounts, and other decorative fixtures. Many pieces are handcrafted by skilled artisans, giving each design a warm, natural character that works well across a variety of interior styles. Rowabi also places strong emphasis on quality, durability, and responsible material choices. Natural materials such as rattan and seagrass are commonly used, while products go through careful testing and inspection to help ensure reliable performance and long-term use. More than simply providing illumination, Rowabi sees lighting as an important part of a home's atmosphere and personality. Through thoughtful design, artisan craftsmanship, and approachable pricing, the brand aims to create lighting that makes everyday spaces feel warmer, more inviting, and more personal.

Living Room Lighting: Warmth and Personality

The living room is often the heart of the home, a gathering space where warmth and personality are essential. Rowabi’s living room lighting collection features everything from rattan and modern pendant lights to flush mounts, semi-flush mounts, and wall sconces. These fixtures are designed to transform everyday moments into glowing memories. Our lighting experts are quick to assist with any inquiries, offering personalized advice to help you select the ideal pieces for your space.

  • Ambient Lighting: Provides overall illumination, perfect for creating a comfortable general light level.
  • Task Lighting: Focused light for activities like reading or puzzles, often achieved with elegant table and floor lamps.
  • Accent Lighting: Highlights artwork, plants, or architectural features with wall sconces or spotlights.

Bedroom Lighting: Serenity and Style

Discover serenity with Rowabi's exquisite pendant lights for the bedroom. These handcrafted rattan masterpieces diffuse a soft, soothing glow, creating an intimate atmosphere perfect for relaxation. The natural textures and organic forms bring a touch of tranquility to your personal sanctuary, elevating your sleep space with our timeless, sustainably sourced lighting designs.

Kidroom Lighting: Soft Glow for Little Ones

Transform your baby’s space with Rowabi’s lighting, where eco-friendly, hand-woven designs create a soft, soothing glow for every lullaby and late-night cuddle. Our kidroom lighting focuses on safety, durability, and creating a comforting environment for your little ones.

Kitchen Lighting: Functionality Meets Ambiance

Discover Rowabi’s kitchen lighting collection, blending modern craftsmanship and natural materials to enhance functionality, ambiance, and style, perfectly tailored for your homes. A tight dome weave, for example, can throw an even, warm pool of light directly onto surfaces, making it ideal for tasks in the kitchen while adding character.

Dining Room Lighting: Setting the Tone

Set the tone for every gathering with our dining room lighting. Our pendant lights are particularly popular for dining areas, offering both focused illumination and a stunning focal point. When choosing a pendant for your dining table, we recommend selecting one roughly 1/2 to 2/3 the width of your table. If you're deciding between two sizes, always go larger to avoid an undersized pendant that might look like an afterthought.

Bathroom Lighting: Damp-Rated for Humid Spaces

For bathrooms and covered patios, Rowabi offers Damp Rated designs from our Outdoor Rattan collection. These fixtures are safe for humid, covered areas with ventilation, though not for direct rain exposure. This ensures you can extend the natural beauty of rattan into moisture-heavy environments without compromising durability.

The Craftsmanship Behind Rowabi Lighting

Every light from Rowabi is crafted with care and passion, ensuring you receive the best in both design and function. Our signature rattan pendant lights, for instance, are handwoven over many hours by expert artisans in Vietnam, often taking up to 136 hours per piece for complex designs. This meticulous process ensures that no two shades are exactly identical, imbuing each fixture with a unique, handcrafted quality that mass-produced lighting cannot replicate.

Premium Materials and Sustainable Practices

We use premium natural materials like rattan, jute, seagrass, and bamboo, which are rapidly renewable plant fibers. Rattan, in particular, regrows far faster than timber and requires no replanting after harvest, making it a lower-impact choice than plastics or metals when responsibly sourced. For outdoor or humid environments, we utilize high-performance fiber resin woven to mimic rattan, providing durability without sacrificing aesthetic appeal.

Versatility in Design and Function

Rowabi lighting offers remarkable versatility to suit various interior styles, including Coastal, Boho, Scandinavian, Organic Modern, and Japandi. The natural tones of our fixtures blend seamlessly, adding warmth and texture without clashing with existing decor.

  • Adjustable Height: All our lights come with adjustable cords, allowing you to customize the hanging height to your specific ceiling elevation.
  • Dimmable Control: Many designs are dimmable-compatible, enabling you to set the perfect mood, from bright task lighting to a soft evening glow.
  • Multiple Size Options: From petite accents to extra-large statement pieces, we offer sizes to fit every scale and room.
  • Plug-in Options: For added flexibility, 36 designs offer a plug-in option, allowing you to install a pendant without needing a ceiling box or an electrician, ideal for rentals or spaces requiring repositioning.
