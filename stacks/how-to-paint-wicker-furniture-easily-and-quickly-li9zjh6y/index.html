Wicker Furniture Painting Service Cost: What to Expect and How to Choose

If you're dissatisfied with the vintage appearance of your wicker furniture or if you have a wicker item that clashes with your home's color palette, repainting it is an excellent option. Understanding the factors influencing wicker furniture painting service cost is crucial for making an informed decision and ensuring you get quality work.

Can You Paint Wicker Furniture?

Wicker furniture remains a favored style, composed of materials such as vines, grasses, and other plants. It is often utilized in outdoor settings, whereas rattan is mainly an indoor material since it can be damaged by moisture. Knowing the distinctions between rattan and wicker is essential for effective care and painting methods. Yes, you can absolutely paint wicker furniture, whether it's made from natural materials like rattan or synthetic fibers. Painting offers a fantastic way to refresh its look, protect it from the elements, and extend its lifespan. For synthetic wicker, it's important to use paints specifically designed for plastic materials.

What Will You Need to Paint Wicker Furniture Yourself?

To paint wicker furniture, you must have a solid grasp of the necessary materials and techniques to make the job efficient and straightforward. Before you rush to get your paint and brush, it's important to check our checklist of required items. While a professional service handles all these details, understanding the process can help you appreciate the value they provide.

Essential Tools and Materials for DIY Wicker Painting

  • Hot glue gun or carpenter's glue for repairs
  • Dishwashing gloves and sponge
  • 5-gallon bucket
  • Stiff brush and handheld or shop vacuum for cleaning
  • Spray sealer and spray paint (with optional spray primer)
  • Dust mask and disposable gloves
  • Drop cloth or cardboard to protect your workspace
  • Mild liquid detergent and bleach (for mold/mildew)

The estimated cost for a DIY project is around $30, with a total time commitment of 8 hours to a full day, including drying times. This demonstrates that while painting wicker is accessible for beginners, it still requires time and specific materials.

How to Spray Paint Wicker Furniture: A Step-by-Step Guide

To spray paint wicker furniture effectively, follow these steps. A professional service like Rowabi will adhere to these best practices, ensuring a durable and beautiful finish.

Inspect and Clean Prior to Painting

It’s common for people to wonder how to properly paint their old wicker furniture. Our first recommendation is to check for any damage or loose strands. If possible, fix them using specialized adhesive or a wicker repair kit before you start. Neglecting repairs can affect the furniture's function, and painting over these issues won't restore its appearance. Use a stiff brush and a vacuum to remove dust and debris from all the nooks and crannies.

Clean and Dry Thoroughly

Use a soap and water solution to clean the furniture and remove any dirt or grease. Rinse well and let it dry completely before applying paint. It’s crucial to ensure there are no dust or dirt particles, as painting over them will lead to an uneven finish. For outdoor wicker or secondhand pieces, add a cup of bleach to the wash water to tackle mildew or mold. Allow the piece to dry completely before moving to the next step.

Mask Any Areas You Wish to Keep Unpainted

A step many overlook is using tape to cover parts of the furniture that shouldn’t be painted, like metal hardware or decorative elements that aren’t easily seen. This will help you conserve paint and reduce the time spent on unnecessary painting tasks. Professionals meticulously prepare the piece to ensure clean lines and protect unpainted components.

Apply Paint in Multiple Thin Coats

Painting wicker is different from painting flat surfaces due to its woven nature. It requires approaching from several angles. Apply light, even strokes with spray paint, walking around the furniture to ensure comprehensive coverage. Allow each coat to dry before applying the next. You may need two to three coats for optimal coverage. Once the top is dry, flip the piece and repeat the process for the underside. This multi-angle, multi-coat approach is key to a professional-looking finish.

Seal for Durability

Once all paint coats are dry, apply a protective sealer. This step is vital, even for indoor pieces, as it safeguards the finish from wear, sun exposure, and moisture, ensuring your freshly painted wicker lasts for years.

Understanding Wicker Furniture Painting Service Cost

The cost to paint wicker furniture professionally can vary significantly based on several factors. At Rowabi, we believe in transparent pricing and high-quality results.

Factors Influencing Pricing

  • Size of the Furniture: Smaller items like chairs or side tables will naturally cost less than larger pieces such as armoires or dining tables.
  • Condition and Repairs: Extensive repairs, such as reweaving damaged sections or fixing structural issues, will add to the overall labor cost. Minor wicker weaving or patching can cost between $150 and $400, while larger repairs like replacing sections might range from $400 to $1,200.
  • Type of Paint and Finish: Specialty paints, such as high-durability enamels or custom finishes, can be more expensive than standard options.
  • Preparation Required: The amount of cleaning, sanding, and priming needed directly impacts labor time and cost.
  • Customizations: Techniques like distressing, antiquing, or custom stenciling add to the complexity and price.
  • Labor Rates: Professional labor for furniture restoration, including wicker repair, can range from $100 to $250 per hour, plus material costs.
